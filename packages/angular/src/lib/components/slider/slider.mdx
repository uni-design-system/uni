## Overview

Reach for a slider when the *position* is the information — volume, opacity,
weightings, price filters — and for a number field when the exact figure is. A
slider is a poor way to enter 47, and a text field is a poor way to feel where
47 sits between 0 and 100. Bind `changed` in a form; `sliding` streams every
frame of a drag and is there for live previews.

One thumb by default, two when `mode="range"`, with a `{ start, end }` value
that reuses the range vocabulary from `uni-calendar`. Stepping is exact decimal
arithmetic from the cdk, shared with the numeric input family, so a `0.1` step
never lands on `0.30000000000000004`. Fill and thumb colour come from the
`variant` role pair; the track, geometry, marks and tooltip resolve from the
theme's `slider` options.

## Accessibility

- Each thumb is a `role="slider"` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, and `aria-valuetext` in the display format — so a money slider says "$500", not "500".
- Arrow keys step by `step`; `Shift+Arrow` and `Page Up`/`Page Down` step by `largeStep`; `Home`/`End` jump to the fences.
- Up and Down always follow the number. Left and Right follow the *picture*, so they mirror in right-to-left layouts.
- In range mode the thumbs are wrapped in a `role="group"` named by `label`, named "minimum" and "maximum", and each thumb's `aria-valuemin`/`aria-valuemax` report the other thumb's position — the wall where it actually is.
- The thumb's hit area is padded to at least 24×24 (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8) even when the visual dot is 16px.
- `label` is required and becomes the accessible name.
- The live region announces only fences and swaps; ordinary movement is already narrated by `aria-valuetext`.

## Do

- Use `changed` for form binding and `sliding` only for live previews.
- Pair a slider with a number field when users need both a ballpark and an exact value.
- Give `marks` labels when the underlying numbers are not meaningful to a reader.

## Don't

- Don't use a slider for a value that needs precision or a wide range — a number field is faster and more accurate.
- Don't put a slider in a scrolling list without testing touch: the track claims horizontal drags.
- Don't rely on the visible readout alone; the value is already in `aria-valuetext`, and duplicating it into the live region is noise.
