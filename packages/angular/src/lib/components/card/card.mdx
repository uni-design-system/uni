## Overview

Content container with a themed frame: the border resolves from the **border
primitive named by its variant** (`borders.primary` … `borders.success`), the corner
radius from the `card` theme options, and an optional elevation shadow. Compose with
Card Header and Card Content for the standard anatomy — or point `options.border` at
a custom primitive to restyle every card in the theme at once.

## States

<StoryExample of={Stories.States} />

`interactive` makes the card something the user acts on — a pointer cursor,
the theme's `hoverColor`, and `activated` on click, Space or Enter — and
`selected` paints it as picked, in the `selectedBorder` frame (`primary` by
default) with an optional `selectedColor` and `selectedElevation`. Both are
classes on the frame, `card-interactive` and `card-selected`, so a variant's
own `&.card-selected` rule reaches them the way it reaches `uni-tag`'s. A
click on a control inside the card's content is the control's, not the card's.

## Pick one: Card Group

<StoryExample of={Stories.PickOne} />

`uni-card-group` is a `radiogroup` of the `uni-card`s projected into it: each
card with a `value` becomes a `role="radio"`, selected and focus-managed by the
group, and a card without one is plain content. The group implements
`FormValueControl<T | null>`, so it binds with `[formField]`, and is laid out as
a grid (`templateColumns`, `gap`, or the `cardGroup` theme options).

## Accessibility

- A plain card is content: no role, no tab stop.
- An option in a group is `role="radio"` with `aria-checked`; the group is `role="radiogroup"`, named by `ariaLabel` or `ariaLabelledBy`, with `aria-required` and an `aria-invalid` gated on touched or dirty.
- One tab stop for the group; the arrow keys both move and select (the APG radio group), so a selection is never a two-step.
- A disabled card is `aria-disabled`, skipped by the arrows and never selected.
- The group is touched once focus leaves it, not while it moves within.
