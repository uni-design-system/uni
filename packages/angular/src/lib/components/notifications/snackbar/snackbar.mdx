## Overview

The Snackbar component is used to give feedback to users after an action has taken place.
It appears near the bottom center of the screen temporarily and floats above the UI to not affect the application's layout.
A side action can be defined (e.g. undoing a process or navigating to the details of a newly created record).

## Accessibility

- `role="status"` announces the message politely, without moving focus away
  from whatever the user was doing.
- The bar renders in the browser's top layer, so it cannot be covered by a
  high `z-index` or clipped by an `overflow: hidden` ancestor — it stays
  readable wherever in the tree you mount it.
- It is a `popover="manual"`, not a modal: the rest of the page stays
  interactive, and a click elsewhere never dismisses the bar out from under
  someone still reading it. Dismissal is the close button, the action button,
  or the timer — which pauses on hover and on focus.
