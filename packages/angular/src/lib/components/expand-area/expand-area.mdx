## Overview

The batteries-included collapsible: a themed surface with a header row, an Expand
Toggle, and a content region wired together — `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`
handled automatically. For custom collapse layouts, compose the lower-level Expand
and Expand Toggle directly.
