## Overview

`− 3 +` for a cart line, a table cell or a seat count: the numeric core with no
field chrome, no label and no room for either. Reach for it inside a row that
already says what is being counted; reach for `uni-number-input` when the field
needs a label, an affix or a currency, and `uni-slider` when the position is
the information.

A separate component rather than a flag on `uni-number-input`, because this
control is defined by what it does *not* have — presets, affixes, expressions,
four stepper layouts — and eight inputs are easier to write correctly than forty
plus a list of which ones to leave alone. The arithmetic, parsing and
hold-to-repeat come from the same cdk primitives, so `1,200` and the keyboard
map behave identically in both.

## Cart line

`deleteAtMin` is the cart pattern in one attribute: at the floor the decrement
becomes a remove affordance, renamed `Remove {label}`, and emits `removed`
rather than stepping. Without it every shop reimplements the same
`value === 1 ? remove() : step(-1)` branch outside the component.

## Sizes

24, 32 and 40px overall — outer height, so an `md` stepper lines up with a 32px
field beside it — with the buttons square at that height. `md` and `lg` clear
the 24×24 pointer target of WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8; `sm` leaves 22px inside its
border, so treat it as the dense desktop option and stay on `md` or larger for
touch.

## Accessibility

- The middle is an `<input role="spinbutton">` named by `label`, with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`. An empty stepper omits `aria-valuenow` and reads "Empty".
- **`label` is never visible and always required.** A cart with six of these needs "Quantity, Blue T-shirt (M)" on each, not six controls all called "Quantity".
- While `editable`, the input is the single tab stop and the buttons carry `tabindex="-1"` as pointer affordances — the keyboard route is the arrow keys. With `editable=false` there is nothing else to focus, so the buttons become the tab stops and the wrapper takes `role="group"` with the name.
- The buttons are square at the component's height, so the 24×24 pointer target of WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 holds at `sm`, `md` and `lg`.
- Arrow keys step, `Enter` commits, `Escape` reverts, blur commits — the same map as `uni-number-input`.
- A `role="status"` region announces fences, clamps and removals. Hold-to-repeat announces once, on release.
- At the fence the matching button is `disabled` rather than a silent no-op. With `deleteAtMin` the decrement stays enabled at `min`, because there it is the one control that still does something.

## Do

- Name the row's subject in `label`, not just the measure.
- Use `deleteAtMin` for cart lines so removal lives in the control rather than beside it.
- Keep `editable` on wherever someone might enter a large quantity — typing 12 beats tapping + eleven times.

## Don't

- Don't use it where a value needs a visible label, a unit or a currency; that is `uni-number-input`.
- Don't wire `removed` to a silent delete. It means "the user asked to remove this row" — confirm or offer an undo as your flow requires.
- Don't give it a wide range. Past a couple of dozen taps a typed field is the faster control.
