## Overview

A scroll container with themed, consistent scrollbars across browsers — thin
Emotion-styled WebKit scrollbars with a `scrollbar-width`/`scrollbar-color`
fallback. It detects scrollable state via ResizeObserver (the `scrollable` output)
and offers `scrollToTop`/`scrollToBottom` for programmatic control.

Three ways to size it: `height` fixes it; `maxHeight` lets it grow with its
content up to a cap, then scroll; `fill` takes the room a flex-column parent
leaves and scrolls within it — the body of a dialog without a wrapper. With none
of them the area is content-sized and never scrolls.
