## Overview

Callout is an anchored coach-mark panel that dims the page and cuts a
spotlight hole around its target — the feature-introduction surface. The
scrim and hole are CSS-anchor-positioned to the target, so the spotlight
tracks scroll, resize, and layout with zero listeners, and the spotlit
element stays genuinely clickable through the hole (or covered, with
`targetInteractive` false). Both surfaces ride the native popover top layer,
above every app z-index.

The panel is a non-modal `role="dialog"`: focus moves in on open, Tab runs a
duet loop over the panel's controls plus the interactive target, and close
restores focus — unless the user moved into the target, where it stays.
Callouts are storage-free by rule: `key` and the `dismissed` output (with a
`reason`) are the "don't show again" hooks, and persistence belongs to the
app.

## Accessibility

- Non-modal `role="dialog"`, labelled by `header` (or `ariaLabel`); `aria-modal` is deliberately absent — the page is dimmed, not removed.
- Initial focus: `[autofocus]` → first action → first focusable → the panel itself.
- Focus restore on close skips users who deliberately moved into the target.
- The spotlight ring is the `variant` color role and pairs with a shape (the ring), not color alone.

## Do

- Point one callout at one action, and let `key` + `dismissed` drive "don't show again"
- Keep the target interactive when the callout invites trying it

## Don't

- Stack multiple callouts at once — sequence them with `uni-tour`
- Use a callout for blocking confirmation; that is a dialog
