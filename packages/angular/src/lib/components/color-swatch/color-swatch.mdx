## Overview

Color Swatch shows a color that is _content_ rather than theme — a paint, a
fabric, a brand ink, a sampled pixel — as a bordered block with its name
beside it. Reach for it wherever such a color is displayed: a row in a list, a
line in a sentence, the image area of a card. It is not for theme colors;
those are tokens.

The block's fill is the given `hex` in every scheme and is never a theme
token. The theme owns the chrome around it, and above all the border — it is
what makes a near-white read as a swatch on a light surface. The swatch has no
opinion about what a color _is_: it takes a `hex`, a `name`, a line of
`detail` and an optional `caption`, and your app decides what goes in them.

## Faces

`chip` sits in a line of text; `tile` stacks the name over the detail, with
the caption beneath; `dot` is the block alone and must be given an
`ariaLabel`.

<StoryExample of={Stories.Faces} />

## Card

`card` is the block alone, edge to edge: the image area of a card, a gallery
slide, a thumbnail. The host sizes the box and draws its own caption, so the
face carries no text — its name, detail and hue are its accessible name
instead.

<StoryExample of={Stories.Card} />

## Without a hex

With no `hex` — or one that cannot be read — the swatch renders the empty
block: the surface with one hairline diagonal, never a placeholder hue. `hex`
accepts what uni-core's `normalizeHex()` does: `#434C5A`, `434c5a`,
`rgb(67, 76, 90)`.

## Accessibility

- A color is never shown by color alone: `chip` and `tile` put the name and detail in the DOM, and their block is `aria-hidden`.
- `card` is `role="img"` named "name, detail, hue" — the hue in words ("a dark slate blue") from uni-core's `nameHue()`; `ariaLabel` overrides it.
- `dot` is `role="img"` and requires `ariaLabel`; without one it logs a dev-mode error and falls back to the derived name.
- Given nothing, the swatch says `emptyLabel` ("No color yet").
- The block prints as it shows (`print-color-adjust: exact`).

## Do

- Keep the border: re-point `colorSwatch.options.border` at another primitive rather than removing it
- Use `card` where the color stands in for a photograph, and put the name in your own caption

## Don't

- Use `dot` without an `ariaLabel`, or anywhere the name is not also on screen nearby
- Put a theme token in `hex` — a swatch is the same sRGB in light and dark
