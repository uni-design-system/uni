The dialog and drawer release: footers that take your own actions, a rich dialog head, and a non-modal overlay drawer.

- **`[dialog-buttons]`, `[drawer-buttons]`**: an `<ng-content>` inside the row. Empty, each renders its pair (Confirm/Cancel, Cancel/Save) as before; project your own buttons and they replace the pair while keeping the row's chrome — padding, gap, `justifyContent`, the divider, `stretch`, `reverseOrder`. Until now importing either component silently discarded whatever a consumer projected, which is why apps carried the slot attribute *without* the import to keep a real footer. A `[dialog-footnote]` / `[drawer-footnote]` projects on the leading edge and pushes the actions to the trailing one. `loading` puts the spinner on the confirm button; on the dialog, `closeOnConfirm` (default `true`) can be turned off so an asynchronous save closes the dialog itself — and a failed one leaves it open with its error.
- **`[dialog-header]`**: a `rich` input renders the projection as a block beside the close button — an eyebrow, a headline, a blurb — instead of one centred line. Two `dialogHeader` theme options dress it: `richColor` (unset by default: the head is part of the sheet, not a pill on it) and `richPadding` (`md`). The new **`[dialog-title]`** directive marks the headline inside a rich head so it alone is the dialog's accessible name; without one the whole block is. The title bar is unchanged.
- **`uni-drawer`**: `modal` (default `true`) on `over` mode. Off, the panel is shown as a `popover="manual"` — still the top layer, where nothing can clip or cover it, but no focus trap, no inert page and no backdrop: a workbench beside the live document it changes, and the reason a toast's Undo stays clickable while a panel is open. Escape still asks to close through `closeRequest` (while focus is inside the panel), so `disableAutoClose` vetoes it as in modal mode; `scrim` is ignored with a dev-mode warning; and `closeOnOutsideClick` (default `false`, reason `'outside'`) opts into dismissal on a click beyond the panel. `DrawerCloseReason` gains `'outside'`.
- **`uni-callout`** (and so `uni-tour`): every retarget re-shows the scrim and panel, so a panel opened by the previous step's click — which entered the top layer after them and covered them — ends up beneath them again, and the next step can point inside it. The panel has to be non-modal for that: a `showModal()` dialog makes everything outside it inert, which no stacking undoes. That is what `modal: false` on the drawer is for.

Base theme: `dialogHeader.options.richColor` (undefined) and `richPadding` (`md`).
