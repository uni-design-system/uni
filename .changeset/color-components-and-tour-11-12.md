Two new components for colors that are content — `uni-color-swatch` and `uni-eyedropper` — a callout whose arrow follows a flipped panel and whose scrim can be feathered and frosted, and four fixes from the Wellsourced adoption.

- **`uni-color-swatch`**: a color that is content rather than theme — a paint, a fabric, a brand ink, a sampled pixel — as a bordered block with its name beside it. Plain inputs: `hex` (any spelling `normalizeHex` reads), `name`, `detail` and an optional `caption`; the app decides what goes in them. Four faces: `chip` (inline), `tile` (two lines), `card` (the block alone, filling its container — an image area, a gallery slide) and `dot`. A color is never shown by color alone: the text faces put the name in the DOM, `card` is `role="img"` named "name, detail, hue", and `dot` requires an `ariaLabel` (a dev-mode error without one). The fill is the given sRGB in every scheme, never a token; with no `hex` it renders the surface with one hairline diagonal.
- **`uni-eyedropper`**: one trigger, two paths. With `images`, a dialog with a loupe over the photograph — pointer or arrow keys move the sample point (Shift = 10px), a click or Enter takes it; with none, the browser's `EyeDropper` where it exists. A sample is the mean of a `(2 × sampleRadius + 1)²` region, never one pixel, and is announced by hue: "Sampled #434C5A, a dark slate blue." Emits `sampled` (`{ hex, source, imageIndex?, point? }`) and `canceled`.
- **uni-core**: `normalizeHex()`, `nameHue()` and `meanColor()` — framework-free, so every binding parses, names and averages a color the same way. `nameHue` tests chroma before hue, so an off-white is not "a light yellow".
- **`uni-callout` / `uni-tour`** (`uni-tour-arrow-flip-and-soft-spotlight-cr.md`): the arrow is drawn from where the panel **landed**, not where it was asked to go — measured on open, on every tour step, and on scroll and resize — so a panel the browser flipped above its target no longer carries its arrow on the far edge, pointing away. `-start` / `-end` placements follow an inline flip too. `uni-popover` and `uni-tooltip` had the same static arrow and get the same fix. The cdk gains `resolvedPlacement()` and `trackPlacement()`. Two optional `callout` theme options: **`spotlightFeather`** (px) fades the scrim toward the target instead of stopping at a line, and **`backdrop`** names a primitive from the theme's `backdrops` scale — the dialog's — whose `background` replaces `scrimColor` and whose `backdropFilter` is applied around the spotlight, never over the target. Both are unset by default; nothing moves.
- **`dialog[uni-dialog]`** (`uni-dialog-animationend-target-cr.md`): a tooltip fading out inside a dialog no longer closes it. Emotion names keyframes by content, so the tooltip's fade carried the dialog's animation name, and `animationend` bubbles; the dialog now acts only on its own event. The same guard is on the drawer, tooltip, alert and snackbar handlers.
- **`BackdropDismiss`** (`uni-dialog-backdrop-vs-popover-cr.md`): a backdrop click that dismisses a menu, dropdown, popover or listbox popup open inside a dialog or modal drawer closes that overlay only — one layer per click, as Escape already was. The next backdrop click closes the dialog.
- **`uni-select`** (`uni-select-arrow-padding-cr.md`): the arrow's padding reserve wins over the input box's own padding rule, so a content-sized select no longer runs its longest option under the arrow.

Base theme: new `colorSwatch` and `eyedropper` entries; `callout.options.spotlightFeather` (0).
