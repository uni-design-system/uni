`'combobox'` joins `ComponentName`, with a base-theme entry: `toggleIcon: 'chevronDown'`, `clearIcon: 'close'`, `selectedIcon: 'check'` (BaseIcons names rendered by `uni-icon` — not Material Symbol ligatures), the listbox surface trio (`listColor`/`listShadow`/`listBorderRadius`), `activeColor`, `descriptionColor`, and `maxVisibleOptions: 8` — a scroll height, never a cap. Field chrome comes from the `input` entry via `uni-input-box`, as everywhere.
