`uni-card` gains states a theme can paint, and `uni-card-group` makes a pick-one grid of them.

- **The frame is an Emotion class**, no longer an inline `[style]`. An inline style outranks every class, so no theme rule could express a state. One thing to know: a consumer stylesheet that was silently losing to the card's inline frame will start winning. Nothing in the base or storybook themes moves.
- **`selected`** and **`interactive`** inputs add `card-selected` and `card-interactive` to the frame — the contract `uni-tag` has had since 11.1 — and the `card` theme entry gains `selectedBorder` (`primary` by default), `selectedColor` (`primary-container` by default — every card already wears the primary frame under the base theme, so the fill is what makes a pick visible), `selectedElevation`, `hoverColor` and `focusRingColor` for the common case; a variant's own `&.card-selected` rule reaches it for anything else. An interactive card emits `activated` on click, Space or Enter, and leaves clicks on its own content's controls alone. `disabled` is read.
- **`[dialog-header]` follow-ups** (`dialog-rich-head-followups-2026-10-22.md`): a `rich` head finds its `[dialog-title]` in the DOM, so a title projected *through* a wrapper component's template — invisible to a content query, and unable to inject the dialog — still names the dialog alone; and with no marked title it defers to the dialog's own `ariaLabel` rather than naming a wrapped sheet by its whole head. The header's close button, in both shapes, follows the dialog's `defaultCloseButton`, as the drawer header's follows the drawer's — a sheet that closes from its footer says `[defaultCloseButton]="false"` once. **`[dialog-buttons]`, `[drawer-buttons]`**: an `alignItems` theme option, default `center`, so a one-line footnote sits on the buttons' centre line instead of stretched to the row's top edge; the pair renders as before.
- **`uni-card-group`** (new): a `role="radiogroup"` over the `uni-card`s projected into it. Each card with a `value` becomes a `role="radio"` whose selection and tab stop the group drives; a card without one is plain content. `FormValueControl<T | null>` (`value`, `disabled`, `touched`/`touch`, `invalid`, `dirty`, `required`, `ariaDescribedBy`), named by `ariaLabel` or `ariaLabelledBy`, `activated` on a pick. APG radio keyboard model: one tab stop, arrows move and select (wrapping), Home/End, Space or Enter. Laid out as a grid from `templateColumns` and `gap`, with defaults in the new `cardGroup` theme entry. Single-select; `multiple` is deliberately not here until a case asks for it.
